# Gage & Hank: Bonk the Brainwash!

A family fighting game created by Gage. The whole family has been hypnotized, and it's up to you to bonk them free!

**Play it here: https://mchoisington.github.io/family-smash-bros/**

Works on iPhone, Android, and computers. Nothing to download and no sign-up.

## How to play

- Pick anyone in the family and fight in the Yard, the Woods, or the Desert.
- **Story Mode:** beat each hypnotized family member to free them.
- **Versus:** play the computer, or two players on one screen.

## Controls

**On a phone:** turn it sideways. Use the joystick to move. Push up to jump, and push up again in the air to double jump. Tap Punch, Kick, or the star Special button when it glows. Hold down and tap Kick to trip someone.

**On a keyboard:**

| | Player 1 | Player 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Move | A and D | Left and Right arrows |
| Jump | W | Up arrow |
| Crouch | S | Down arrow |
| Punch | F | K |
| Kick | G | L |
| Special | H | ; |

Xbox and PlayStation controllers work too.

## Add it to your Home Screen

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the three dots, then Add to Home screen.
